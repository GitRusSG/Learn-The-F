Section A: Algebraic Fractions (5 marks)

1. Simplify:   (x² - 9) / (x² + 5x + 6) [2]
Tip: Factorise top (difference of squares) and bottom (trinomial) separately, then cancel.
2. Express as a single fraction:   2/(x+1) + 3/(x-2) [2]
Tip: LCD = (x+1)(x-2). Multiply each fraction to get common denominator.
3. Simplify:   (2x² - 8) / (x² - 4x + 4) [1]
Tip: Factor top as 2(x²-4), bottom as (x-2)².

Section B: Completing the Square (5 marks)

4. Express x² + 8x + 10 in the form (x + p)² + q. State p and q. [2]
Tip: Half of 8 is 4. Square it: 16. So x²+8x+16 = (x+4)². Then adjust the constant.
5. Solve by completing the square:   x² - 6x + 5 = 0 [2]
Tip: (x-3)² - 9 + 5 = 0 → (x-3)² = 4 → x-3 = ±2
6. What is the minimum value of x² - 6x + 11? [1]
Tip: Complete the square first. The minimum of (x-p)²+q is always q (since the squared part ≥ 0).

Section D: Hard Formulas & Mixed (5 marks)

9. Make x the subject:   y = (2x + 1)/(x - 3) [2]
Tip: Multiply both sides by (x-3), expand, collect x terms on one side, factor out x.

(b) Write down the equation of the line of symmetry. [1]

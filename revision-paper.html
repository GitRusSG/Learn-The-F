Detailed Solutions — Full Working

Q1. Simplify (x²-9)/(x²+5x+6)

Step 1: Factor the top: x²-9 = (x+3)(x-3) [difference of squares]

Step 2: Factor the bottom: x²+5x+6 → numbers that × to 6 and + to 5 → 2,3
= (x+2)(x+3)

Step 3: Cancel (x+3) from top and bottom:
= (x-3)/(x+2) ✓

Q2. Express 2/(x+1) + 3/(x-2) as a single fraction

Step 1: LCD = (x+1)(x-2)

Step 2: First fraction: 2(x-2)/LCD = (2x-4)/LCD
Second fraction: 3(x+1)/LCD = (3x+3)/LCD

Step 3: Add tops: (2x-4)+(3x+3) = 5x-1

Answer: (5x-1)/((x+1)(x-2)) ✓

Q3. Simplify (2x²-8)/(x²-4x+4)

Top: 2x²-8 = 2(x²-4) = 2(x+2)(x-2)

Bottom: x²-4x+4 = (x-2)²

Cancel one (x-2): = 2(x+2)/(x-2) ✓

Q4. Express x²+8x+10 as (x+p)²+q

Half of 8 = 4. Square it = 16.

x²+8x+10 = (x²+8x+16) - 16 + 10 = (x+4)² - 6

p = 4, q = -6 ✓

Q5. Solve x²-6x+5=0 by completing the square

Half of -6 = -3. Square = 9.

x²-6x+5 = (x-3)² - 9 + 5 = (x-3)² - 4

Set = 0: (x-3)² - 4 = 0

(x-3)² = 4

x-3 = ±2

x = 3+2 = 5  or  x = 3-2 = 1 ✓

Q6. Minimum value of x²-6x+11

Complete the square: (x-3)² - 9 + 11 = (x-3)² + 2

Since (x-3)² ≥ 0 always, the smallest y can be is 0 + 2 = 2.

Minimum value = 2 (occurs when x = 3) ✓

Q7. y = (x-1)(x-5)

(a) x-intercepts: Set y=0 → (x-1)(x-5)=0 → x=1 or x=5

(b) Line of symmetry: Midpoint of 1 and 5 = (1+5)/2 = x=3

(c) Turning point: Sub x=3: y=(3-1)(3-5)=(2)(-2)=-4 → (3,-4)

Q8. y = -(x+2)(x-4)

(a) x-intercepts: x=-2 and x=4

(b) The coefficient of x² is negative (the - in front), so the parabola is ∩ shaped → Maximum

Q9. Make x the subject: y = (2x+1)/(x-3)

Step 1: Multiply by (x-3): y(x-3) = 2x+1

Step 2: Expand: xy - 3y = 2x + 1

Step 3: Collect x terms on left: xy - 2x = 3y + 1

Step 4: Factor out x: x(y-2) = 3y + 1

Step 5: Divide: x = (3y+1)/(y-2) ✓

Q10. y = (x-p)²+q with turning point (2,-5)

(a) The turning point of (x-p)²+q is at (p,q). So p=2, q=-5.

(b) Line of symmetry passes through the turning point vertically: x=2

(c) Minimum — the coefficient of x² is positive (no negative sign), so the parabola is ∪ shaped and the turning point is the lowest point.
